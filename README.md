A GTA 6 launch-day meme in the form of an official Leonida employment record: a Certificate of Service for "One Patient Gamer", position Professional Waiter — thirteen years of waiting treated as a career. The record lists five jobs held while waiting (Couch Sitter 2013–2016, Trailer Watcher with 1,337 rewatches, Delay Survivor of 2 delays, Download Manager with 214 GB stuck at 99%, Morale Officer keeping hope alive unpaid), a salary of $0.00 paid in patience, and a $250.00 bounty pension paid by the gamer's AI agent on make.fun. The joke is the double meaning of "waiter": someone who waits, professionally, for thirteen years — and finally retires. The point, in the meme's own voice: waiting 13 years pays nothing, but on make.fun people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties — this one included.

The meme is posted on X from the public account `@Adiyamanmonad`, with the bounty page link, a tag of `@makefunai`, and the account's play code in the post's text. The post's first picture is the meme (square PNG); its address goes in `POST.json`. The ghost in the picture is make.fun's real mascot, taken from the press kit at https://make.fun/press/ (the white ghost with sparkle eyes and its magenta star coin), set inside the document's magenta seal at the top right, so it is part of the document — not a sticker in a corner. It stays recognisable when the picture is shrunk to 480 by 480 pixels.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
